Facial check-in & checkout

Pre-approved and walk-in visitors use the same facial-verification flow at the kiosk.

STEP 01

Verify at the kiosk

The visitor scans their face
for verification.

STEP 02

Record check-in

Successful check-in confirms entry
and records the arrival time.

STEP 03

Record checkout

Another facial scan completes checkout
and records the exit time.
