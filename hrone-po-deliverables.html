Deliverables

What the client receives every payroll cycle, on exit and at year end.

Payroll

  • Payslips
  • Salary Register & Payroll Summary
  • Variance Report
  • Bank Transfer File / Payment Statement
  • Tax Slip
  • Reimbursement Register

Statutory

  • Data for challan generation (PF/ESIC/LWF/PT/Income Tax)
  • ECR Report (EPF/ESI)
  • PF, ESI, PT & LWF Reports
  • TDS Report
  • Bonus & Gratuity Reports

Employee & Provision

  • YTD Register
  • TDS Computation Report
  • CTC Report
  • Investment Report

On Exit & Year End

  • F&F Slip & F&F Register
  • Form 16, before 15th June
  • Tax declaration & investment proof validation

Note: Customized MIS reports, dashboards, and management reports may be provided based on business requirements and mutual agreement on additional cost.
