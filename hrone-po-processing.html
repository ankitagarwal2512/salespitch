Core Payroll Processing

Monthly Payroll Processing

HROne shares the monthly payroll calendar per the agreed TAT and processes inputs provided and approved by the Client — attendance, LOP, joiners, exits, salary changes, arrears, incentives, reimbursements, loans, and other earnings or deductions.

If the Client has procured HROne services, payroll inputs are captured automatically from the HROne system — reducing manual data handling and turnaround time.

Every input category shown here is validated by HROne before processing.
