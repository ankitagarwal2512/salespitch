Attendance Kiosk
Attendance when nobody has a company device.
The problem
  • Employees needed an accessible, consistent way to record attendance.
  • Limited availability of individual devices restricted attendance capture.
  • Manual processes created errors and processing delays.
  • Attendance records lacked centralised visibility.
Studio solution
  • A shared kiosk-based attendance application.
  • A simplified attendance-recording experience for employees.
  • Attendance entries centralised within a common system.
  • A quick, convenient process designed for operational locations.
Results
  • Attendance recorded in seconds on a shared device.
  • More consistent records with central visibility.
  • Fewer attendance errors and processing delays.
  • Less manual attendance administration.
