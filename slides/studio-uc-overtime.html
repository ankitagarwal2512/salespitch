Overtime Requests
Overtime that reaches payroll on time.
The problem
  • Manual overtime requests arrived with missing or inconsistent information.
  • Approval delays affected timely payroll processing.
  • Limited tracking made pending requests hard to monitor.
  • Maintaining a reliable audit trail was challenging.
Studio solution
  • A structured digital overtime request form.
  • Standardised information submitted by employees.
  • Systematic review and approval workflows.
  • Centralised overtime records for tracking and reference.
Results
  • Simpler submission and tracking of overtime requests.
  • Better approval turnaround and accountability.
  • Accurate, consistent information for payroll teams.
  • Stronger record-keeping and audit readiness.
