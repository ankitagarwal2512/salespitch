Visitor Management

Manage scheduled visits and walk-ins, with facial verification and recorded entry and exit.

Pre-approved visitors

Host schedules the visit. Approval triggers an access code shared by email and WhatsApp.

Walk-in visitors

Register by QR code or visitor form. Details go for approval, then the visitor receives an entry code.

Facial check-in & checkout

Visitors scan their face at the kiosk. The system records entry and exit times automatically.
