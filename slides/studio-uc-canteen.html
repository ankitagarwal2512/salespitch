Canteen Management
How do you plan meals across shifts, lines and rest areas?
The problem
  • Meal requirements varied by location, assembly line, shift, shop number and rest area.
  • Count updates needed approval and timely communication to canteen managers.
  • Overtime and visitor requirements needed separate handling.
  • Historical changes needed tracking without table-level snapshots.
Studio solution
  • A master-data-driven app for standard meal, snack, tea and coffee quantities.
  • Approval workflows for updating standard counts.
  • Notifications to canteen managers whenever approved counts change.
  • Separate overtime and visitor workflows with shift-based or custom time slots.
Expected results
  • Central meal planning across locations, shifts and operational areas.
  • Revised requirements reach canteen managers reliably.
  • Record-level audit visibility on changes to standard quantities.
  • Simpler additional meal and refreshment requests.
