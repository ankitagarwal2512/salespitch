Stationery Management
How do you run a 500-item inventory across every department?
The problem
  • An inventory of roughly 100–500 items needed structured master data.
  • Departmental requests carried multiple items, quantities and business justifications.
  • Approved quantities often differed from requested quantities.
  • Distribution and receipt needed reliable tracking, including partial fulfilment.
Studio solution
  • A central inventory of item codes, types and names.
  • Administrators and IT teams maintain the stationery master.
  • A single form for HODs to request multiple items for their department.
  • GA approval with quantity modification, distribution recording and HOD receipt acknowledgement.
Expected results
  • Standardised stationery requests across departments.
  • Visibility into requested, approved and distributed quantities.
  • Central inventory and request records for easier monitoring.
  • Accountability through approval and receipt confirmation.
