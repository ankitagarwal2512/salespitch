Uniform Management
How does a large manufacturer run uniform logistics for its entire workforce?
The problem
  • Six distinct processes — standard distribution, departmental bulk, replacements, paid uniforms, annual size collection, new joinees.
  • Quantities and employee acknowledgements needed structured tracking.
  • Partial fulfilment complicated the records.
  • Paid requests needed payment verification and approval.
Studio solution
  • One application covering all six uniform processes.
  • GA teams notify eligible employees of collection schedules.
  • Distributors and HODs record issued items and capture employee confirmation.
  • Replacement approvals, payment verification, annual size collection and new-joinee allocation.
Expected results
  • Central visibility into requests, allocations, quantities and collection status.
  • Accountability through employee acknowledgement of received items.
  • Simpler standard, replacement, paid and new-joinee requests.
  • Consolidated size data for annual vendor purchasing.
