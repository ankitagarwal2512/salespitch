Seating Management
Who sits where, and who approved it?
The problem
  • Seat availability and departmental ownership needed central tracking.
  • Employee-to-seat assignments had to stay tied to department and location.
  • HODs needed controlled access to allocate, release and exchange seats.
  • Seat requests needed multi-level approval and visibility into capacity.
Studio solution
  • A seat master holding seat number, location, department and availability status.
  • An employee-seat mapping table linking employees to assigned seats.
  • HOD-scoped allocation, release and swap requests within their departments.
  • Two-level approval through reporting manager and GA team, with department-wise summaries.
Expected results
  • Visibility into allocated, available and disabled seats.
  • More accurate employee seating records.
  • Standardised allocation, release and swapping processes.
  • Department-wise seating capacity monitoring for admin teams.
