Project Feedback
Employee and manager views, side by side.
The problem
  • Employee and manager feedback was collected through different channels.
  • Inconsistent formats made responses difficult to compare.
  • Manual consolidation increased administrative effort.
  • Management lacked a unified view of project feedback.
Studio solution
  • Structured feedback workflows for employees and for managers.
  • Standardised information collected from both groups.
  • Centralised responses for review and analysis.
  • A consistent process for field validation and approval.
Expected results
  • Consistent feedback collection across employees and managers.
  • A consolidated project-feedback view for management.
  • Less manual compilation and follow-up effort.
  • Simpler comparison and analysis of feedback.
