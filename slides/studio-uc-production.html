Production & Delivery Tracking
Where is the order, and who owns the next step?
The problem
  • Sample and full-size orders passed through multiple production stages.
  • Fragmented tracking caused missed updates and repeated follow-ups.
  • Teams lacked clear visibility into production and delivery status.
  • Final field requirements needed business validation.
Studio solution
  • A centralised order-tracking application.
  • Workflows for both sample and full-size production orders.
  • Production and delivery milestones built into the flow.
  • Clear ownership defined at each stage to support delivery continuity.
Expected results
  • End-to-end visibility across sample and full-size orders.
  • Clearer identification of pending stages and responsible owners.
  • Fewer manual status follow-ups and less coordination effort.
  • Better view of production progress and delivery readiness.
