Vendor & Hawker Management
Four processes, thousands of records.
The problem
  • Four separate vendor and hawker processes needed digitising.
  • Large record lists made vendor and hawker selection time-consuming.
  • Users had to scroll manually to locate the records they needed.
  • Limited filtering capability affected user efficiency.
Studio solution
  • Four digital forms covering vendor and hawker management.
  • Standardised capture and processing of operational information.
  • Centralised vendor and hawker records for easier access.
  • Streamlined record selection and workflow navigation.
Results
  • Centralised, structured vendor and hawker information.
  • Standardised workflows across all four processes.
  • More accessible operational records.
  • Less manual effort locating and processing records.
